Floor plan · on this phone only

Scan your flat, get the plan

Walk through your home and tap the corners of each room on the floor. You get a clean 2D plan with room areas and wall lengths, saved as a PDF. Nothing leaves this phone.

How it works

  1. Point at the floor and move the phone slowly until the ring appears on it.
  2. Tap "Add corner" at each corner of the room, where the wall meets the floor, going round in order.
  3. Close the room and name it. Walk to the next room and keep going: one session for the whole flat keeps the rooms lined up.
  4. Mark doorways by tapping both sides of each opening.
  5. Finish to see the plan, fix names, and save the PDF.

Checking whether this phone can scan in AR…

Works in Chrome on Android phones with Google Play Services for AR. Good light and slow movements give the best walls.
